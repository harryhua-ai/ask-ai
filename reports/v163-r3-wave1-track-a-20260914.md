- Reworked list and detail actions so high-frequency actions are visible and low-frequency actions remain in a menu.
- Added a lightweight list-level `同步记录` panel and kept full history/health/diagnostic content on the detail route.
- Reframed health and generation copy around connection, reliability, knowledge availability, freshness, retrieval/index consistency, and technical evidence. Unknown coverage is shown as `暂不可评估`, without inventing a percentage.
- Added direct detail actions for editing, knowledge settings, truth inspection, and repair.
- Preserved the ordinal-zero generation sentinel as a technical migration record and prevented it from being presented as in-service business volume.
